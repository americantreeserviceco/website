If you have a dead, declining or hazardous tree, you may be thinking it can wait until spring. In many cases, winter is actually the better time to take it down. Here's why many Front Range homeowners schedule removals between November and March, and what to plan for.

## Why winter works well for tree removal

- **Firm or frozen ground.** Equipment rolls over hard ground with far less rutting and lawn damage than in a soggy spring.
- **Dormant landscapes.** Perennials, shrubs and garden beds are dormant, so there's less to protect and less to replant.
- **Better visibility.** Without leaves, crews can see the structure of the tree and plan rigging and cuts precisely.
- **Lighter cleanup.** Bare branches mean less debris to chip and haul.
- **Easier scheduling.** Spring and summer fill up fast. Booking in winter usually means more flexibility on dates.
- **Ready for spring planting.** Remove the old tree and grind the stump in winter, and you're ready to plant a replacement in spring.

## Signs a tree should come down

Not every problem tree needs to be removed. Pruning, cabling or treatment can save many trees. Removal is usually worth discussing when you see:

- A tree that is dead or more than half dead
- Large cracks, splits or cavities in the trunk
- Decay or fungal growth at the base, or root damage from construction
- A sudden new lean or heaving soil
- An ash tree with serious [emerald ash borer](/blog/emerald-ash-borer-front-range.html) damage
- A tree that's too close to the house, foundation or utility lines

## Before you book: permits, HOAs and utilities

- **Public trees:** Trees between the sidewalk and the street often belong to the city. Denver and many other Front Range cities regulate work on public right-of-way trees, so check with your city forester before any work.
- **HOA rules:** Many HOAs require approval before a tree is removed or replaced.
- **Power lines:** Trees growing into service or distribution lines require special precautions. Never attempt this yourself.

## What about the stump?

Removal usually leaves a stump at ground level. [Stump grinding](/services/stump-grinding/) takes it below grade so you can replant, lay sod or pave. Grinding is often done the same day or shortly after removal.

## Why not DIY?

Felling a mature tree near a home is one of the most dangerous jobs in residential work. Trees rarely fall exactly where you expect, and dead wood can break unpredictably. Professional removal with rigging, climbing and the right insurance protects your home, your neighbors and you.
