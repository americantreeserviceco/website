How it works

One door opens each day from December 13 to 24. Each tip is a short winter tree-care reminder, and some come with a festive question. After December 24, all twelve tips stay open.

  • A door a day

    Doors open at midnight Mountain Time. Each tip covers watering, mulch, young trees, bark or pruning plans, with a source linked where one applies.

  • Festive questions

    Tell us about your favorite tree, the most recent tree you planted, or a holiday tree display you love.

  • Real answers for real trees

    These tips are general reminders, not a diagnosis. For a property-specific concern, ask for an arborist assessment.
