## Our El Niño Emergency Storm Services

### 1. Tree Removal
Severe storms can cause trees to fall or become hazardous. Our team safely removes 
these trees to protect your property and ensure the safety of your family and 
neighbors.

### 2. Tree Trimming and Pruning
After a storm, it's essential to assess and prune your trees to remove damaged 
branches and restore their natural shape. We provide expert trimming to keep your 
trees healthy and attractive.

### 3. Stump Grinding and Removal
Large storm-damaged trees often leave behind stumps. Our professional stump grinding 
service ensures that your property looks neat and tidy while promoting healthy 
growth.

### 4. Emergency Tree Repair
We understand that storm damage can happen at any time. Our emergency repair 
services are available 24/7 to address urgent tree-related issues.

### 5. Prevention and Preparedness
Our team can also help you prepare your trees for El Niño conditions. We offer 
consulting services to ensure your trees are as resilient as possible before the 
storm hits.
