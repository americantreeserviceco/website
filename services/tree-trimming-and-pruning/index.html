Our Pruning Services

  • Structural pruning: selective removal of branches to build a strong framework, especially on young trees, so they grow with a sound form and are less likely to split or fail in wind and heavy snow.
  • Crown thinning: removing select live branches throughout the canopy to let in light and air and to reduce weight and wind resistance, without changing the tree’s overall size or shape.
  • Crown raising: removing lower branches to lift the canopy, creating clearance over sidewalks, driveways, roofs, and sight lines.
  • Crown reduction: shortening branches back to suitable side branches to reduce a tree’s height or spread, for example near buildings or utility lines. Done correctly, this is very different from topping, which damages trees.
  • Preventive maintenance: regular inspections and light pruning to remove dead, diseased, or weak branches before they become hazards, especially ahead of winter storm season.
