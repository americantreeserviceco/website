About Us

Founded in 1993, American Tree Colorado provides residential and commercial arborist services across the Denver Metro area. Led by Troy King, an ISA Certified Arborist with 46 years of hands-on experience, we specialize in complex, high-risk, and environmentally sensitive tree care. We focus on diagnosing, preserving, and protecting trees whenever possible, without compromising safety.
