About Us

Founded in 1993, American Tree Colorado provides residential and commercial arborist services throughout the Denver Metro area. Our work is led by founder Troy King, an ISA Certified Arborist with 46 years of hands-on experience.

Our Mission

Our approach is simple: preserve first, remove last, and never compromise safety or integrity. We take the time to understand each tree and site, then recommend responsible care that gives trees every reasonable chance to thrive.

Experience That Puts Preservation First

In 2012, Troy was asked to assess a historic Colorado tree connected to the White House. When removal was considered, he developed a custom preservation treatment instead. The tree responded so well that the treatment approach continues to be referenced, reflecting our belief that careful diagnosis can reveal options beyond removal.
