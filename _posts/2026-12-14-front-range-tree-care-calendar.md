Trees on the Front Range deal with a lot: dry winters, wet spring snowstorms, summer hail, wind and pests like emerald ash borer. The good news is that most problems are easier and cheaper to prevent than to fix. Use this calendar to plan your tree care for 2027, whether you're in Golden, Lakewood, Arvada, Wheat Ridge, Denver or Boulder.

## Winter (December to February)

- **Structural pruning.** Dormant trees show their structure clearly and heal well in spring. [Why winter pruning works](/blog/dormant-season-pruning-colorado.html).
- **Tree removals.** Firm ground and dormant landscapes make winter a great time to take down dead or hazardous trees. [Winter removal guide](/blog/winter-tree-removal-colorado.html).
- **Winter watering.** Water during dry spells on days above 40°F. [How much and how often](/blog/winter-watering-trees-front-range.html).
- **Ash tree decisions.** Assess ash trees for emerald ash borer and plan spring treatment or removal. [EAB guide](/blog/emerald-ash-borer-front-range.html).
- **Remove holiday light wraps** before spring growth. [Decorating tips](/blog/holiday-lights-trees.html).

## Spring (March to May)

- **Watch for spring snowstorms.** March is typically one of the snowiest months along the Front Range, and wet spring snow on leafing trees breaks branches. Inspect trees after each storm.
- **Finish pruning before leaf-out,** especially elms.
- **Plant new trees** once the ground thaws. Choose species that fit the space and add diversity to your yard.
- **Start EAB treatments** on ash trees worth protecting, applied by a licensed professional.
- **Mulch** 2 to 4 inches deep over the root zone, kept away from the trunk.

## Summer (June to August)

- **Deep water during heat and drought,** especially young trees and evergreens.
- **Check after hail and thunderstorms** for broken limbs, torn bark and hangers. Our [rapid hazard response](/services/rapid-hazard-response/) team handles urgent problems.
- **Watch for pests and disease:** sudden wilting, leaf spots, sawdust or holes in bark.
- **Avoid pruning elms** while elm bark beetles are active.

## Fall (September to November)

- **Prepare for early snow.** Front Range storms can arrive while trees still have leaves. [What to do before and after](/blog/early-snow-front-range-trees.html).
- **Fall planting** works well while soil is still warm.
- **Winter root fertilization** feeds roots heading into dormancy. [Learn more](/blog/winter-root-fertilization.html).
- **Begin winter watering** once fall rains end.
- **Hire before you need to.** Our guide on [how to choose a tree service](/blog/how-to-choose-a-tree-service-colorado.html) explains what to ask.

## Any time of year: don't ignore hazards

Cracked limbs, a new lean, fungus at the base or branches over the roof can't wait for the right season. Use our [homeowner's safety checklist](/blog/how-to-tell-if-your-tree-is-dangerous-homeowners-safety-checklist.html) and call if something looks wrong.
