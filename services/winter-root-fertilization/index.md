# Full-Zone Root Nourishment™ – Deep Root Tree Fertilization

Colorado's dry climate, challenging soil conditions, and unpredictable weather can make it difficult for trees to thrive. American Tree Colorado's **Full-Zone Root Nourishment™** service delivers essential nutrients throughout the root zone to support stronger roots, healthier foliage, and long-term tree vitality.

Our seasonal deep root fertilization program supports trees during spring growth, summer heat and drought stress, and fall root development. By improving nutrient availability and supporting healthy soil conditions, we help trees maintain their natural resilience against environmental stress.

## Seasonal Root Nourishment

- **Spring Root Nourishment (January–April):** Supports root activity and prepares trees for the growing season by addressing nutrient needs as soil conditions become favorable.
- **Summer Root Nourishment (May–August):** Helps maintain tree vitality during Colorado's hot, dry months by supporting nutrient availability and healthy root function.
- **Fall & Dormant Root Nourishment (September–December):** Supports root health and nutrient reserves as trees transition into winter dormancy.

## Why Choose American Tree Colorado?

- **Diagnostic Approach**: We inspect soil moisture, root space, and tree health before recommending fertilizer, ensuring you only treat what your trees actually need.
- **Precision Subsurface Application**: Professional-grade injectors deliver nutrients throughout the feeder root zone where trees can absorb them.
- **Front Range Expertise**: Formulated for Colorado's alkaline soils, low organic matter, and semi-arid conditions.
- **Honest Recommendations**: We will tell you if your tree needs winter watering, compaction relief, or disease management rather than unneeded fertilizer.
- **Licensed and Insured**: Led by ISA Certified Arborist Troy King with comprehensive coverage for your property.
